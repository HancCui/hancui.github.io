I am a PhD in the WestlakeNLP Group at Westlake University and Zhejiang University, advised by Yue Zhang.

Previously, I earned both my bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Science from the Harbin Institute of Technology.

My primary research interests lie in the area of Natural Language Processing. Previously, I mainly focused on reasoning by the language models in an explicit and quantifiable manner, including explanation graph generation (EG3P), and reasoning over heterogeneous data (HProPro).
